# Company and product context

Collected 2026-09-07 so later agents do not scrape the marketing site again.

## Product

Cozmo AI positions itself as the AI operating system / workforce for property & casualty:

- **Intake:** first notice of loss, photos, documents
- **Dispatch:** route contractor, SLA
- **Field:** mitigation/repair tracking
- **Estimate:** scope and price, carrier-ready (Xactimate / Cotality / Symbility language)
- **Settle:** pay and reconcile with an audit trail

Site: https://www.hellocozmo.ai/  
Vision product page: https://www.hellocozmo.ai/see (photos, video, documents, dash-cam, persistent visual memory)  
YC: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cozmo-ai  

Control layer they brand: **APE (Agent Protocol Engine)** — shared state, policy, explainable actions.

Integrations mentioned in marketing: Encircle, Dash, Albiware, ServiceTitan; core systems like Guidewire on the carrier side.

## Why a floor plan primitive belongs here

Restoration estimates are mostly **quantities from geometry**: wall area, floor area, baseboard length, opening counts. Today a human sketches that in Xactimate after walking the loss. Field photos already exist. If an agent can produce a dimensioned sketch, estimate drafting gets a structured input instead of “look at these 40 JPEGs.”

That is the through-line from the JD (“draft the carrier-ready estimate from field photos”) to the problem sentence in `discussion.md`.
